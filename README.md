# 🥩 Eyethu Butchery Management System

An inventory and point-of-sale system for a butchery. It keeps live stock, deliveries, daily trading and sales totals in sync, so the front counter and the back office always see the same numbers.

## Features

- **Dashboard:** today's revenue, estimated sales and low-stock count, with quick actions and a recent-activity feed.
- **Point of Sale:** searchable product grid (by name or SKU) showing price and live stock, a current-sale panel, rand discounts and a payment method selector, with subtotal and total due.
- **Products and categories:** manage items such as beverages, fresh meat and bakery, sold by unit, kg, bottle or loaf.
- **Receive stock:** record a delivery and update stock levels.
- **Stock control:** adjust stock and review stock records.
- **Business day:** open and review the trading day.
- **Staff accounts and system users:** role-based access with login and logout.
- **Reports and business intelligence:** sales and stock reporting.
